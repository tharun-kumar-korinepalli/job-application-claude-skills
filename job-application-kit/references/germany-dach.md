# Germany, Austria and Switzerland (DACH)

Read this whole file for any application in German or to a company based in DACH, even when the posting is in English. Most rejections of foreign applicants in Germany are not about skills. They are about a missing Zeugnis, an unclear work permit line, a US-style resume that looks incomplete to a German HR person, or a letter that opens with "Hiermit bewerbe ich mich".

## The three documents

A German Bewerbung is a set:

1. Anschreiben (cover letter), one page
2. Lebenslauf (CV), one to two pages, tabular, with a photo or not (below)
3. Anlagen (attachments): Arbeitszeugnisse, Abschlusszeugnisse, certificates, sometimes a Deckblatt

Missing Zeugnisse is the single most common reason a foreign application is put aside. Recruiters expect proof for every degree and, where they exist, references from every employer. If the person has none, say so in one line of the letter and offer reference contacts.

## Lebenslauf

### Format

- Title at the top: "Lebenslauf". Not "Curriculum Vitae", not "Bewerbung".
- Tabular. Left column dates, right column content. Two columns is normal here and German ATS (Personio, SAP SuccessFactors, Softgarden, d.vinci, rexx) parse it fine. Keep it a real table or tab stops, not text boxes.
- Reverse chronological. The old chronological order is gone.
- One page for juniors, two for everyone else. Three is too many.
- A4. Calibri, Arial or similar. 11 pt body.
- Date and signature at the bottom is traditional. Optional now. Harmless to include, and older HR people still like it: "Würzburg, 16.09.2026" plus a scanned signature or the typed name.

### Photo

Since the AGG (2006) a photo cannot be required and many large companies now write "bitte ohne Foto". It is still common and still expected by many Mittelstand firms. Rule:

- Posting says no photo: none.
- Large corporate, tech, international, English posting: optional. Omitting is normal.
- Mittelstand, public sector, healthcare, hospitality, sales, anything customer-facing: include one.
- If included: professional, neutral background, business dress for the industry, head and shoulders, top right, roughly 3.5 by 4.5 cm. Never a cropped holiday photo, never a selfie.

Ask the user. Do not assume.

### Persönliche Daten block

Standard lines, top of the CV, next to the photo:

```
Name               Vorname Nachname
Anschrift          Straße Nr., PLZ Ort
Telefon            +49 ...
E-Mail             name@domain.de
Geburtsdatum       12.03.1994 in Chennai (optional)
Staatsangehörigkeit  indisch (optional; useful with a permit line)
Aufenthaltsstatus  Blaue Karte EU, unbefristete Arbeitserlaubnis (only for non-EU applicants, and only if it helps)
```

Street address is expected in Germany, unlike the US. Date of birth is optional since the AGG but still widely given; omitting it can make an HR reader wonder. Marital status, religion and number of children are gone. For non-EU applicants a clear permit line removes the question the recruiter would otherwise have to ask. Never invent a status.

### Sections and order

```
Persönliche Daten
Kurzprofil (optional, 3 lines, for experienced people)
Berufserfahrung
Studium / Ausbildung
Praktika (for students; otherwise fold into Berufserfahrung)
Kenntnisse
   Sprachen (with CEFR: Deutsch C1, Englisch C2, Tamil Muttersprache)
   IT / Fachkenntnisse
   Führerschein (only if the job needs it)
Weiterbildung / Zertifikate
Publikationen (if any)
Engagement / Interessen (optional, one line, only if it says something)
Ort, Datum, Unterschrift
```

### Entries

```
03/2023 – heute     Research Associate, Institut X, Kitzingen
                    Angewandte KI für Robotik, Betreuung von Masterarbeiten
                    • Aufbau eines RAG-Dokumentensystems für 4 Forschungsgruppen ...
                    • ...

10/2020 – 02/2023   M.Sc. Artificial Intelligence, Technische Hochschule Y, Würzburg
                    Abschlussnote 2,1 · Masterarbeit: "Titel" (Note 1,3)
```

- Date format MM/JJJJ – MM/JJJJ. "heute" for current. "seit 03/2023" is also accepted.
- Location for every entry.
- Berufserfahrung entries carry bullets with results, same rules as writing-bullets.md. German HR increasingly prefers results over duty lists.
- Education: include Abitur or the foreign equivalent in one line (school, city, year, grade if good). German readers expect the school line.

### Gaps

Any gap over roughly three months should have a line: "Elternzeit", "Stellensuche", "Weiterbildung (Kurs X)", "Pflege eines Angehörigen", "Reise". A labelled gap is fine. An unlabelled gap gets asked about or gets the application dropped.

### Foreign degrees

- Give the original title, then the German equivalent in brackets if it is recognised: "B.E. Mechanical Engineering (entspricht Bachelor of Engineering)".
- Grades: give the original scale and, if it has been calculated, the German equivalent using the modified Bavarian formula: "CGPA 9,37/10 (entspricht ca. 1,2)". Show the formula once if asked. Do not round in the applicant's favour.
- Mention anabin status if the degree is listed as "H+" or has a ZAB Zeugnisbewertung. Public sector and regulated professions will ask.
- Translate the degree certificate. Certified translation for public sector and regulated jobs; a plain translation is enough for most private companies.

### Language levels

CEFR only: A1 to C2. "Verhandlungssicher" (roughly C1/C2), "fließend" (B2/C1), "gut" (B1/B2), "Grundkenntnisse" (A2). State the honest level. A German-language interview will test it in the first minute. If German is B1 and the role wants B2, say so in the letter with the plan.

## Anschreiben

One page, DIN 5008 letter layout. Formal by default. Match "Du" only if the posting uses "Du" throughout.

```
Vorname Nachname
Straße Nr.
PLZ Ort
Telefon · E-Mail

Firma GmbH
Frau/Herr Vorname Nachname (Abteilung)
Straße Nr.
PLZ Ort

                                                        Ort, 16. September 2026

Bewerbung als [Stellentitel wie in der Anzeige], Kennziffer [123]

Sehr geehrte Frau Nachname,

[Absatz 1: Aufhänger, konkret. Warum diese Firma, warum diese Stelle. Kein "hiermit".]

[Absatz 2: Zwei bis drei Belege mit Ergebnis, auf die Hauptanforderungen gemünzt.]

[Absatz 3: Passung zum Unternehmen und Team, was Sie einbringen wollen.]

[Absatz 4: Eintrittstermin, Kündigungsfrist, Gehaltsvorstellung falls verlangt, Einladung zum Gespräch.]

Mit freundlichen Grüßen

Vorname Nachname

Anlagen: Lebenslauf, Arbeitszeugnisse, Abschlusszeugnisse
```

Rules:
- Subject line bold, no "Betreff:" prefix. Use the exact job title from the posting and the reference number.
- Named recipient if the posting gives one or LinkedIn shows the hiring manager. "Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren" is the fallback, and it is a weak one.
- Never open with "Hiermit bewerbe ich mich", "Mit großem Interesse habe ich Ihre Anzeige gelesen", or "Ich bin ein motivierter Teamplayer".
- 250 to 400 words.
- Gehaltsvorstellung when asked: one sentence, gross annual, a number or a narrow range. "Meine Gehaltsvorstellung liegt bei 68.000 Euro brutto im Jahr." Leaving it out when asked is treated as not following instructions.
- Eintrittstermin: "Ich kann zum 1. Januar 2027 beginnen" or "Meine Kündigungsfrist beträgt drei Monate zum Quartalsende".
- Sign-off "Mit freundlichen Grüßen". Not "Liebe Grüße", not "Beste Grüße", except in a "Du" startup.
- Non-native German: keep sentences short. A short correct letter beats a long one with errors. If the user writes the letter in English and wants it in German, translate for meaning, not word by word, and keep the technical terms in English.
- Signature: scanned image or just the typed name. Both are fine in a PDF.

### Common German phrases that read as filler

Cut: "hochmotiviert", "Teamplayer", "belastbar", "flexibel", "kommunikationsstark", "Ich bringe Leidenschaft mit", "Ihr Unternehmen ist ein Global Player", "Ich bin überzeugt, dass ich die ideale Besetzung bin". Replace each with a fact.

## Anlagen and the Bewerbungsmappe

Order inside the PDF:
1. Anschreiben
2. (Deckblatt, optional: name, target job, photo, contact. Skip it unless the industry expects it.)
3. Lebenslauf
4. Arbeitszeugnisse, most recent first
5. Abschlusszeugnisse: university, then school
6. Certificates, courses, language certificates

Merge into one PDF, usually under 5 MB (some portals say 10 MB, some 2 MB; check). Name it `Bewerbung_Vorname_Nachname_Firma.pdf`. Many portals want separate uploads instead; then name each file: `Anschreiben_Vorname_Nachname.pdf`, `Lebenslauf_Vorname_Nachname.pdf`, `Zeugnisse_Vorname_Nachname.pdf`.

Scan Zeugnisse cleanly, straight, readable, no phone shadows.

### Arbeitszeugnis

German employers write coded references. "stets zu unserer vollsten Zufriedenheit" is a top grade; "zu unserer Zufriedenheit" is mediocre; "bemühte sich" is a warning. When the user has a German Zeugnis, read it and flag anything that reads below "gut" so they can ask for a correction (they have a legal right to a fair and benevolent Zeugnis).

Foreign applicants without Arbeitszeugnisse: ask former employers for a reference letter on letterhead with dates, role and a few lines on performance. State in the letter: "Arbeitszeugnisse sind in Indien unüblich; Referenzschreiben und Kontakte meiner früheren Vorgesetzten lege ich bei." That one sentence prevents a quiet rejection.

## English applications to German companies

Common in tech, research and international firms. Keep the German expectations that matter:
- Attach Zeugnisse anyway.
- Put city and country, and a permit line for non-EU applicants.
- Photo optional. Follow the posting.
- The CV can follow the international format in resume-format.md. Do not sign it.
- Cover letter in English but with a subject line and reference number.
- Dates and numbers can stay in English format.

## Austria

Same set of documents. More formal, titles matter: "Sehr geehrter Herr Mag. Huber", "Dipl.-Ing." on the CV if held. Photo very common. Salary: postings by law show the minimum collective-agreement salary; state your expectation above it with reasoning. Sign-off "Mit freundlichen Grüßen". Lebenslauf often two pages even for juniors.

## Switzerland

- No ß. Write ss everywhere.
- "Freundliche Grüsse" as sign-off.
- Pensum: state the percentage you are applying for if the posting lists one ("Pensum 80–100 %").
- Permit: state it plainly ("Aufenthaltsbewilligung B", "EU citizen, no permit needed", "requires permit" for non-EU; many Swiss firms will not sponsor).
- CV two to three pages is accepted. Photo common. Referenzen (named people with phone) often listed at the end, with their consent.
- Salary discussed in CHF gross annual, 13 monthly salaries is standard.
- Multilingual postings: answer in the language of the posting.

## Timing and follow-up

- Apply within the first two weeks. Many postings stay online long after the shortlist is drawn.
- Silence for four weeks is normal. A polite Nachfrage after three to four weeks is fine: two sentences, asking about the status and confirming continued interest.
- Bewerbungsfrist in the posting is a hard line for public sector, a soft one for private companies.
- Expect a first interview, then a second with the team, sometimes a Probearbeitstag. Contracts usually carry a six-month Probezeit.

## Quick checklist for DACH

- Anschreiben one page, named recipient, subject with reference number, no "hiermit"
- Gehaltsvorstellung and Eintrittstermin present if asked
- Lebenslauf tabular, reverse chronological, with location on every entry
- Photo decision made deliberately
- Address, and a permit line for non-EU applicants
- Gaps labelled
- Language levels in CEFR and honest
- Foreign degree shown with German equivalent
- Zeugnisse attached, or the missing ones explained
- One PDF or correctly named separate PDFs, under the size limit
- Numbers in German format in German documents (68.000 Euro, 1,5 Mio., 18 %)
