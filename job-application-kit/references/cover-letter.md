# Cover letters, motivation letters and application emails

Half of recruiters skip the letter. The other half use it to break ties and to check whether the person can write. In Germany the Anschreiben is still expected by most companies. Either way it has to be short, specific and sound like a person.

## Length and shape

- One page. 200 to 350 words. Four paragraphs, sometimes three.
- Same font and header as the resume.
- Date, recipient block and subject line only where the market expects it (DACH yes, US no; see the regions files).
- Named recipient if it can be found from the posting, LinkedIn or the company site. Otherwise "Dear Hiring Team" or "Dear [Team name] Team". "To whom it may concern" is dead.
- Sign-off: "Kind regards" or "Best regards". "Sincerely" in the US. "Mit freundlichen Grüßen" in German.

## The four paragraphs

1. Hook. One or two sentences that could only be written for this company and this role. A product they ship, a problem the posting names, a talk someone gave, a paper, a launch, a customer you both know, a referral. If the person has a direct connection (uses the product, knows someone there, worked in the same niche), it goes in the first line.

2. Proof. Two or three achievements with results, chosen because they match the top must-haves. Brief context for each. This is not a summary of the resume. It is the argument for why the resume is worth opening.

3. Fit. Why this company, what about the team or mission or stage is attractive, and what the person would want to work on. Concrete. "I have followed your move into SME lending since the March launch and the underwriting problem you described in the engineering blog is close to what I built at X."

4. Close. Plain and confident. Availability, notice period, start date, salary expectation if asked. One sentence inviting a conversation. No "I would be honoured", no "I am confident I am the perfect candidate".

## Openers that get the letter binned

- "I am writing to express my interest in the position of..."
- "I am excited to apply for..."
- "Please find attached my resume for..."
- "Hiermit bewerbe ich mich um..."
- "With great enthusiasm..."
- Any sentence that starts with "As a passionate..."

## Openers that work

- "Your posting asks for someone who has taken a recommendation system from prototype to production. I did that twice at [company], and the second one still serves 2 million users."
- "I have used [product] daily for two years, and the export flow you are hiring someone to rebuild is the one I complain about most."
- "[Name] on your data team suggested I write to you about the ML engineer opening."
- "Your NeurIPS paper on contradiction detection is close to my master's thesis, and the follow-up questions it left open are the ones I would like to work on."

## Handling the elephant

If there is an obvious question, answer it in one or two sentences, without apology, usually in paragraph three or the close.

- Gap: "I took 2024 off to care for my father. I kept up with the field through [concrete thing] and I am fully available from January."
- Career switch: "I spent eight years in mechanical engineering before moving into ML. That background is why I understand the sensor data your product depends on."
- Overqualified: "I am deliberately looking for a hands-on role after five years of managing. I miss building."
- Relocation: "I am based in Madrid and can relocate to Munich within six weeks at my own cost."
- Language: "My German is B2 and I use it daily at work; I am taking C1 classes this term."
- Underqualified on one item: "I have not run Kubernetes in production. I have deployed on ECS and Docker Swarm and ramping on k8s is the first thing I would do."

## Tone by company type

| Company | Tone | Example line |
|---|---|---|
| Startup | Direct, conversational | "I have spent three years building exactly this." |
| Corporate | Professional, measured | "My experience in enterprise integration matches the objectives in the posting." |
| Public sector, university | Formal, criteria-driven | "I address each of the selection criteria below." |
| Research lab | Precise, references work | Name the paper, the method, the open problem. |
| Agency, creative | Personality forward | "Your rebrand for X is why I am writing." |
| German Mittelstand | Formal, sincere, "Sie" | See germany-dach.md |

## Motivation letter (Motivationsschreiben, PhD, scholarships, master's)

Longer (up to one and a half pages), more about direction than proof. Structure: what draws you to this programme or group specifically, what you have done that prepares you, what you want to do there, why you are the right fit for their way of working. Name the professor, the group, the paper, the course. Generic admiration is the fastest way to lose the reader.

## Email application (no separate letter)

When the posting says "send your CV to jobs@..." and no letter is requested:

Subject: Application: [Job title] (Ref [number]) – [Full name]

Body: three short paragraphs at most, the hook and one proof, attachments named, sign-off with phone number. Attach PDF only. Name the files properly.

## The read-aloud test

Before delivering, read every sentence and ask two things: could this sentence go in a letter to a different company, and would the person say it out loud in an interview? If either answer is no, rewrite or cut. Then run the letter against human-voice.md.

## Verify list

End every letter with a short list for the user: contact name spelling, company legal name vs brand name, job title as written in the posting, reference number, every number in the letter, the date.
