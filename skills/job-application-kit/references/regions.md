# Regional conventions outside DACH

Ask which market the application is for. A document that is normal in one country looks wrong in another. DACH has its own file: germany-dach.md.

## United States and Canada

- One page under about ten years. Strict. Two pages for senior people.
- No photo, no DOB, no nationality, no marital status. Including any of these signals unfamiliarity and creates liability for the recruiter.
- City and state, no street address.
- "Resume" is the word. "CV" means the academic document.
- Work authorisation: only mention if it removes a question ("Authorized to work in the US without sponsorship") or if the posting asks.
- Cover letters are optional at most companies and read by hiring managers more than recruiters. Keep to 200 to 300 words.
- US spelling. Dates "Jan 2023 – Present".
- Salary expectations: give a range only if asked; many states require the posting to show a range.
- Canada: same as the US, but bilingual (English/French) can matter in Québec and federal roles; A4 is not used.

## United Kingdom and Ireland

- CV, two pages. Personal statement of three to four lines at the top is normal.
- No photo, no DOB, no marital status. City only.
- Right-to-work line for non-UK citizens: "Skilled Worker visa, valid to 2028" or "Irish citizen".
- UK spelling: optimised, programme, organisation.
- Dates "Jan 2023 – Present".
- Cover letter expected for most roles outside tech. Public sector often wants a supporting statement against the person specification, point by point.
- References: omit or "available on request". Named referees only when asked.
- Grades: UK degree classification (First, 2:1). For foreign degrees give the original and, if known, the UK NARIC/ECCTIS equivalent.

## Australia and New Zealand

- Two to three pages is normal, even for mid-level.
- No photo, no DOB. Visa or residency status is expected: "Australian permanent resident", "Working holiday visa, valid to".
- Two named referees with title and phone are common, with their consent, or "Referees available on request".
- Government jobs: written responses to selection criteria, usually two pages, STAR format per criterion.
- Australian spelling (as UK). Dates "Jan 2023 – Present".
- Cover letter one page, expected.

## France

- CV one page for juniors, two max. Photo optional but still common. Age or DOB often given. Nationality optional.
- Sections: État civil, Formation, Expérience professionnelle, Compétences, Langues, Centres d'intérêt.
- Lettre de motivation expected, formal register, one page, ends with a formal closing formula.
- Education carries more weight than in the US; name the school and the diploma precisely.

## Netherlands, Belgium, Nordics

- Short, direct, no photo in NL and the Nordics; Belgium mixed.
- English is fine for international roles. Dutch or the local language for local roles.
- Cover letter short and concrete, often a single page or an email.
- Dutch: "sollicitatiebrief". Personal data minimal.

## Spain, Italy, Portugal

- Photo common, DOB common, nationality often given.
- One to two pages. "Carta de presentación" or "lettera di presentazione" expected for many roles.
- Italy: a GDPR consent line at the bottom of the CV is customary ("Autorizzo il trattamento dei miei dati personali ai sensi del GDPR 679/16").
- Technical terms stay in English.

## India

- One to two pages. Photo only if asked. DOB, nationality and sometimes marital status are often included but are not required by MNCs; leave them out for international employers.
- CGPA and percentage are given. State the scale.
- "Declaration" line and signature at the bottom is an old convention; skip it for modern employers.
- Notice period and current CTC / expected CTC are asked in almost every form; have the numbers ready.
- For Indian applicants going abroad: convert the format to the target country. The Indian resume with DOB, father's name, hobbies and a declaration reads badly in the US and Europe.

## Middle East (UAE, Saudi, Qatar)

- Photo, nationality, DOB and visa status are commonly requested. Include them when asked, not otherwise.
- Two pages. English. Cover letter optional.
- Driving licence and current location are often asked.

## Japan

- Two fixed-form documents: rirekisho (personal history, standard form, photo required) and shokumu-keirekisho (work history, free format). Do not send a Western resume alone.
- English resume may be requested in addition for foreign companies.

## Academic and research roles anywhere

See academic-cv.md. The academic CV ignores page limits and follows discipline conventions.

## Moving between formats

Keep the master document in one format and generate the target-country version from it. The content stays, the wrapper changes: page size, personal data block, photo, length, spelling, date style, sign-off, letter layout. Never send a DACH Lebenslauf with a photo and DOB to a US company. Never send a one-page US resume to a German Mittelstand firm without Zeugnisse.
